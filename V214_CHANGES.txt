KeySuite V2.14 Changes
======================

1. End Suction material sequence
- Enforced: CI SS SS MS / CI SS SS GP / CI CI SS MS / CI CI SS GP / SS 304 / SS 316.
- CI BR SS MS and CI BR SS GP remain excluded.

2. System Description and KeyPLC routing
- Coupled KeyPLC descriptions start with c/w on row 1.
- Rows 2 onward begin with exactly four spaces.
- Direct Product > KeyPLC quotations do not include c/w.
- Split pump wiring and pressure-transmitter wiring into separate lines.

3. System header alignment
- Model / Item, Qty and Unit Price share equal label, control and help-row heights.

4. System/Pumpset quotation transfer
- Removed internal Pumpset and Control Panel BOM summaries from customer quotation descriptions.
- Quantity wording is Set for one and Sets for more than one.

5. New quotation isolation
- Each New Quotation receives a new working-session identifier.
- Quotation items, customer/pricing context and System/Pumpset working drafts are isolated from the previous quotation.
- Saved quotations retain their assembly-session identifier when reopened.

6. Automatic control panel
- The System BOM sizes KeyPLC by total pump quantity and nearest adequate motor kW.
- Auto-selected control-panel Qty is fixed at 1 and Qty/Unit Price fields are read-only with a filled background.
- Manually added Product > KeyPLC items remain editable.

7. Manifold defaults and automation
- Defaults: GI / Flange 16 Bar / 2 Pumps / Common.
- Non-default selections use the existing pink highlight.
- Automatic Manifold size and standard price follow pump DN and total BOM pump quantity.
- Connection uses the lowest supported rating strictly above zero-flow shut-off pressure: Thread 8 Bar, Flange 10 Bar or Flange 25 Bar.
- When no curve is available, Flange 16 Bar is used.
- Assembly description includes c/w and four-space continuation indentation.

8. Automatic GWS tank selection
- CHC series up to 10: 24 L.
- CHC series 12–16: 35 L.
- CHC series 35–90: 100 L.
- CHC series 120–150: 200 L.
- CHC series 200: 300 L.
- Tank pressure rating is the lowest available rating strictly above the pump's zero-flow shut-off pressure.

9. Database
- No new Supabase migration is required for V2.14.
