KeySuite V3.8.6

1. Dashboard Quick Pump Selection
   - Shows the Most Suitable model plus all Alternative Models returned by CHC and ES.
   - Each model displays Motor kW + Pole + pump efficiency, e.g. 7.5kW 4P 78.4%.
   - Recommended and alternative model names are clickable and open the actual pump curve using the current Dashboard duty.
   - Available Series starts expanded by default; each family can still be collapsed manually.
   - Preserves the V3.8.5 repeated-duty reset/retry behavior.

2. Most Suitable selection — CHC and ES
   - Applies only to the single recommended/Most Suitable model.
   - First: normal hydraulically valid candidate set. ES torque-exceeded candidates cannot be recommended.
   - Then rank by BEP flow group relative to required Duty Flow:
       Group 1: -5% to +3%
       Group 2: -10% to +8%
       Group 3: -18% to +13%
       Outside Group 3: lower priority.
   - Within the same best group, highest pump efficiency at the actual duty point wins.
   - Existing engineering/catalog tie-breaks are retained after BEP group and efficiency.

3. Alternative Models — CHC and ES
   - No fixed number of alternatives.
   - Show every hydraulically eligible model except the currently selected model.
   - Required flow must be within the full-speed / maximum-impeller pump curve.
   - Duty head must be at or above the head at the pump's maximum-flow endpoint.
   - Duty head may be up to 5% above the full-speed / maximum-impeller curve head at the required flow.
   - Alternatives are sorted small-to-big by the established CHC/ES catalog sequence, not by BEP group or efficiency.

4. ES shaft torque visibility
   - A hydraulically eligible ES model that exceeds its shaft torque limit remains visible in Alternative Models.
   - It is marked with a red torque warning, e.g. "Torque Exceeded – use ES 50-26G" when the matching G model exists.
   - Torque-exceeded ES models are excluded from the Most Suitable recommendation pool.

5. Product > End Suction
   - Adds a Curve button matching the CHC Product workflow.
   - Adds one top configuration row: Material / Seal Type / Seal Material / Elastomer.
   - Removes duplicate per-model dropdowns for those fields.
   - Gland Packing automatically uses Graphite seal material.
   - Changing the top controls updates an open ES Product curve/configuration.

6. No database migration required.
