KeySuite V2.35 — Tyre Coupling BOM Editor Fix

Base: V2.34

- Replaced the Tyre coupling editor mutation flow with an error-safe, deferred render flow.
- Keeps the same Coupling BOM item ID when changing Flexible, Pin & Bush, and Tyre.
- Prevents automatic coupling refresh from taking control during a user edit.
- Keeps Selected Type, Model, F/H Arrangement, and Delete usable even when sizing or pricing fails.
- Persists Coupling mode and error state through local/remote Assembly saves.
- Deleting Coupling suppresses automatic recreation until Restore Coupling is used.
- Adds safe handling for missing Tyre data, unavailable suitable models, and pricing exceptions.
