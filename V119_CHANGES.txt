KeySuite V1.19

- Category list now reliably switches the selected category into the right panel.
- Holding a protected Category field for 3 seconds now enables editing and unlocks that field directly.
- CHC and GWS keep separate USD/RMB multipliers; multiplier fields require a 3-second hold and save automatically.
- Added Normal and Rare percentages to every CHC and GWS category rule.
- Added Common / Many / Rare to every CHC, CHCS, CHCN and GWS pressure price.
- Many preserves the V1.18 formula. Common adds Normal before Transport. Rare adds Normal and Rare before Transport.
- Existing products default to Many to preserve current selling prices.
- V1.08 PDF output remains frozen and unchanged.
