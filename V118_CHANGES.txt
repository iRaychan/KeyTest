KeySuite V1.18

- Category Pricing Rule now places CHC and GWS as vertically stacked product buttons.
- Each category stores separate CHC and GWS margin, transport and optional pricing components.
- Margin, transport, commission, set discount, final discount and fuel controls require a 3-second hold before editing.
- Commission, set discount, final discount and fuel charge can be included/excluded by checkbox.
- Category displays read-only Price List currency rates: USD, RMB and MYR.
- Price List now maintains independent USD, RMB and MYR prices and automatically saves USD/RMB rates.
- Final costing uses the highest MYR equivalent from USD, RMB or MYR before the category pricing rule.
- Added GWS Tank master models and 10 Bar / 16 Bar / 25 Bar price lists.
- Added Product > GWS Tank and quotation pricing support.
- Product > CHC mobile layout uses one model per row with equal Curve/PDF/Quote controls.
- Selector Export / Share PDF remains visible in phone landscape view.
- Quotation items start collapsed; Export Center labels are shortened to PDF and Item 01, Item 02, etc.
- Saved quotations retain their own internal pricing customer. A newly selected customer only appoints the customer for a new quotation.
- Printed customer and printed contact names remain editable without changing the saved pricing customer.
- The V1.08 quotation PDF layout remains frozen and unchanged.
