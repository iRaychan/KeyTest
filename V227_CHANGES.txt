KeySuite V2.27 Changes

1. Pumpset > System now reads Motor data from the nested Pumpset BOM.
2. The KeyPLC panel pump count follows the total Motor quantity.
3. The KeyPLC panel rating follows the highest Motor HP.
4. Motor HP is converted to kW and matched to the exact or nearest larger KeyPLC panel rating.
5. If Motor data is unavailable, the existing pump motor-kW sizing remains as the fallback.
6. Pumpset set quantity multiplies the nested Motor quantity.
7. The automatically selected panel Motor Rating remains editable in System BOM.
8. Panel Type remains editable as Indoor or Sheltered.
9. The grouped Pumpset remains one System BOM item with its nested BOM retained.
