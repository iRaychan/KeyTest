KeySuite V2.28 Changes

COUPLING PRODUCT
- Added Product > Coupling.
- Selection fields: Coupling Type, ES Pump Model, Motor HP, Motor Pole, Coupling Model, Pump Bush and Motor Bush.
- Automatically reads the ES pump shaft and Motor frame/shaft mapping from the supplied workbook.
- Automatically recommends the smallest suitable coupling by shaft capacity, torque and maximum speed.
- Supports Pin & Bush and Tyre Coupling.
- Direct Quote action uses the Coupling Category Pricing Rule.

COUPLING PRICELIST
- Added Price List > Coupling.
- Collapsible Currency panel following the existing CHC/ES method.
- USD, RMB and MYR price input.
- Separate Pin & Bush, Tyre and Bush tabs.
- RMB source prices seeded from the supplied Coupling workbook.
- Blank workbook prices remain zero and editable.
- Tyre Coupling source price combines Tyre + Pump Bush + Motor Bush before applying the pricing rule once.

ASSEMBLY
- Coupling Assembly action routes only to Assembly > Pumpset > Coupling.
- Pumpset BOM allows Coupling Type, Model, Pump Bush and Motor Bush selection.
- Changing the Pump or Motor context recalculates the recommended coupling and its price.
- Pin & Bush Assembly description is exactly: c/w<TAB>Pin & Bush Coupling
- Coupling model remains visible in the BOM but is not included in that Assembly description.

PRICING & DATABASE
- Added COUPLING to Category Pricing Rule.
- Added ks_products_coupling and secure save RPCs.
- Added separate Coupling USD and RMB multipliers.
