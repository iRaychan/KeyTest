KeySuite V1.21

Quotation workflow
- Removed Customers → Quote / Quoted selection.
- New quotations start with no customer; select the customer inside Quotation.
- Customer, Customer Name, Printed Customer and Printed Contact lock with darker fields after the first item is entered.
- Saved quotations remain editable.
- Added Seal button. Sealed quotations are read-only and PDF controls appear only when sealed.
- Hold Sealed for 5 seconds to unseal; countdown is shown and the action is recorded in quotation audit history.
- Added Revise. A sealed quotation is duplicated as -v01, -v02, etc. Customer and original Date remain locked; Revision Date is editable.
- Revised copies must be sealed before PDF controls appear.
- Final remaining quotation item can still be deleted; removing all items unlocks customer fields on a normal saved quotation.

Pricing rule resolution
- Pricing is resolved strictly by Quotation Customer → Customer Category → Product Series (CHC or GWS) → Rarity.
- Dealer CHC and Dealer GWS use independent product-rule values and checkbox states.
- Many, Common and Rare formulas remain separate.
- Sealed quotations cannot be repriced or receive new items.

PDF
- V1.08 print/PDF layout and print document structure are unchanged.

Database
- No Supabase SQL migration is required for V1.21.
