KeySuite V2.20 Changes
======================

1. Key > Company
   - Adds a dedicated Company page in the Key Dashboard.
   - Shows the signed-in company information and protected commercial settings.
   - The Company page and pricing-factor editor are visible only to the Owner.

2. Company Assembly and Quotation factors
   - Adds Assembly and Quotation tabs under Key > Company.
   - Existing Commission, Set Discount, Final Discount and Fuel Charge settings move to Quotation.
   - Assembly has its own Commission, Final Discount and Fuel Charge settings.
   - Assembly deliberately excludes Set Discount.

3. Category Pricing Rule simplification
   - Category Pricing Rule now contains only Margin, Normal, Rare and Transport.
   - Company factors are no longer edited or displayed in the Category editor.
   - Manual quoted-value calculation continues to use the protected Quotation company factors in the background.

4. Separate Assembly and Quotation pricing
   - Products and automatic BOM components added to Assembly use Assembly factors.
   - Direct Quote actions use Quotation factors.
   - System and Pumpset transfer recalculates every source-priced BOM component with Quotation factors.
   - Sealed quotations are not recalculated when Company factors change.
