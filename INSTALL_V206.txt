KeySuite V2.06 Installation
===========================

Prerequisite
- Your Supabase database must already be upgraded through V2.05.
- V2.06 has no new database schema changes.

Deployment
1. Keep a copy of the current deployed files.
2. Extract KeySuite_V2.06_GitHub.zip.
3. Preserve your existing working config.js.
4. Upload the V2.06 files to the deployment root.
5. Hard refresh the browser with Ctrl + Shift + R.
6. If installed as a PWA, close and reopen it after refreshing.

Verification
- ES Price List: scroll vertically and horizontally; the header and Model column must remain visible.
- ES Price List: confirm the top and bottom horizontal scrollbars stay synchronized.
- Product > CHC: open CHC 2 and CHC 64 curves and confirm points at 2 and 64 m³/hr.
- Product > CHC: press Assembly once and Quote once; each action must add exactly one item.
- Quotation: save an item with zero price, then confirm Seal is blocked until all item prices are entered.
