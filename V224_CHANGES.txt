KeySuite V2.24 Changes
======================

Product > Motor Model
- HP selection list.
- Pole selection list: 2, 4, 6 and 8 Pole.
- Efficiency selection list: IE1 to IE5, default IE3.
- Generated Model, Description and Assembly Price summary.
- Assembly remains routed to Pumpset → Motor.

Motor Price List
- Currency & Multipliers follows the CHC/ES collapsible panel method.
- Currency panel starts collapsed.
- Efficiency and Pole are outside the currency panel.
- No search field.
- Efficiency defaults to IE3.
- Table displays HP rows for the selected Efficiency and Pole.
- Price input displays the selected USD, RMB or MYR currency.

Database
- No new V2.24 migration is required.
- V223_SUPABASE_MIGRATION.sql remains required for the Motor module.
