KeySuite V2.18 Changes
======================

1. Quotation-scoped Assembly drafts
   - New quotations no longer display drafts from previous quotation sessions.
   - Empty and legacy drafts are excluded from the active quotation draft list.
   - Supabase Assembly records now store quote_session_id.

2. Assembly/System description indentation
   - The first line begins with c/w.
   - Every continuation line begins with exactly one tab and zero spaces.
   - The same indentation is preserved in Quotation and PDF output.

3. KeyPLC wiring wording
   - The two separate wiring rows are combined into one row for pumps and pressure transmitter wiring.

4. Multiple quotation templates
   - Company-wide templates for Owners/Admins.
   - Personal/dealer templates visible only to the owner.
   - Template logo, company details, colour, font, price columns, defaults, signature visibility, header/footer, bank details and PDF filename.
   - Template copy, preview, default, disable and delete controls.
   - Sealed quotation template snapshots preserve historical output.
