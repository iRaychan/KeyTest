KeySuite V2.38 Changes

1. Pumpset BOM Couplings now distinguish Auto Coupling and Manual Coupling records by independent stable BOM item IDs.
2. Deleting Auto Coupling suppresses only the automatic record and shows Restore Auto Coupling.
3. Product > Coupling > Assembly adds a Manual Coupling even while Auto Coupling is suppressed.
4. Auto and Manual Couplings may coexist; deleting either record leaves the other records unchanged.
5. Restore Auto Coupling recreates only the automatic selection and preserves all Manual Couplings.
6. Pumpset-to-System transfer preserves all Coupling BOM records and identifies the preferred resolved Coupling.
7. Pump-related capacity display uses selected units first. Metric units display as, for example, 50 m³/hr @ 50 Mtr.
8. Nonmetric capacity display includes metric conversion, for example, 600 L/m (36 m³/hr) @ 100 Ft (30.5 Mtr).
9. Capacity parsing accepts the new bracketed metric format and the L/m, L/min, Ft, ft, Mtr and m aliases.
10. Quotation History now filters exact year/month combinations. Each year has 12 month checkboxes plus Select All Months and Clear Months.
11. Existing Customer Name, authorised User filtering, and database-enforced quotation ownership remain unchanged.

KeyAI remains paused and is not included in this Version 2 build.
