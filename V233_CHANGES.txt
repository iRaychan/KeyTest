KeySuite V2.33 Changes

1. Pumpset Coupling BOM
   - Tyre, Pin & Bush and Flexible remain editable.
   - Tyre model and F/H arrangement can be changed and persist through automatic refresh.
   - Unsuitable manual selections remain visible with a reason instead of being silently reset.

2. Quotation Coupling Shaft Validation
   - Pump Shaft and Motor Shaft remain editable.
   - Inputs use the non-default warning colour when a shaft exceeds the selected FCL or bush maximum.

3. Margin Protection
   - Blank or 0% Category Margin blocks additions to Pumpset, System and Quotation.
   - Existing BOMs remain visible, but transfer is blocked until the rule is corrected.

4. End Suction Price List
   - Model | Rarity | Material Selection | Selected Currency Price.
   - Material selection loads and saves the exact material/currency price.

5. Quotation Item Behaviour
   - Existing items collapse when a new item is added.
   - Only the newly added item expands and scrolls into view.
   - Motor, Control Panel, Tank and Manifold do not show Capacity controls.

6. Capacity Defaults
   - Flow defaults to m³/hr.
   - Head defaults to Mtr.
