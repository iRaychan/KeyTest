KeySuite V2.34 Changes

Tyre Coupling BOM stability fix
- Prevents repeated Coupling recalculate/render/autosave loops when Tyre is selected.
- Keeps the Coupling BOM item ID stable when changing Flexible, Pin & Bush, and Tyre.
- Recalculates Tyre model and F/H Bush arrangement once per actual Pump/Motor context change.
- Preserves the selected Coupling type and manual Tyre model/arrangement during render and autosave.
- Keeps Selected Type, Model, F/H Arrangement, and Delete controls available after Tyre selection.
- Retains Tyre mode with a validation reason when no suitable model is available.

Coupling delete and restore
- Deleting the Coupling no longer causes it to be recreated immediately.
- A Restore Coupling button is displayed after intentional deletion.
- Changing or adding a Pump/Motor re-enables automatic Coupling sizing.
